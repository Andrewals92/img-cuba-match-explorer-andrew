# Data Governance

## Sources
Community outcome data must be voluntarily self-reported. Program catalog data must come from official/permitted sources. Do not scrape or republish proprietary Residency Explorer data.

## Status labels
Records may be `unverified`, `pending`, `verified`, or `rejected`. Verification is evidence of the reported event reviewed by an authorized moderator, not a guarantee of every profile field.

## Aggregation
- detailed profile medians: n >= 5 distinct applicant cycles
- recent program/week interview activity: n >= 3 reports
- program-level match display: n >= 3 matches

Review thresholds periodically as the community grows.
